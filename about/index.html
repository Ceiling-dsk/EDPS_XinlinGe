Research & education

I am a Ph.D. student in Learning Sciences at the University of Utah (UofU), fortunately advised by Prof. Chenglu Li. My research interests are human-centered AI-enabled educational systems engineering, where I explore how to design, build, and study intelligent systems that responsibly enhance human learning. Prior to my Ph.D. journey, I earned my bachelor's degree in Mathematics from Beijing Normal University (BNU) and my master's degree in Mathematics Education from University College London (UCL).

Beyond academia, I bridge the gap between educational theory, industry practice, and frontline teaching. I am a passionate educator and have been teaching mathematics since 2016. Driven by the potential of AI in education (AIED), I previously led a data company in Shanghai, China, where we specialized in engineering and delivering high-quality math reasoning datasets for AI enterprises.

While I’ve come a long way, I’m still learning, still growing, and still discovering who I want to become.
